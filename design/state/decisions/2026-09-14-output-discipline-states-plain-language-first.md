## Claim
`AGENTS.md` § *Output discipline* also states a wording rule, not only a length rule: say what
happened before what it is called, so a reader is not left to translate a field name, an enum, a
boolean, or an exit code — the exact identifier stays beside the meaning where auditing or acting
on it needs it. This is the rule `Get-NextOrientation.ps1` and `RepoAliases.ps1`'s plain-language
summaries cite.
