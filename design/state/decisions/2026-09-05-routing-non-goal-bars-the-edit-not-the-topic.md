## Claim
`design/00-brief.md` § *Non-goals* keeps "Model routing and tier policy are not changed" unchanged in its original terms and adds that the line bars this project from editing `AGENTS.md` § *Model, effort, and review budget*, the vendor alias list and § *Command routing* — not from the topic existing elsewhere. Taking a command's mechanical half out of a session altogether, so that no model runs it rather than a cheaper one, is a different project's to define; this brief neither authorises that here nor forecloses it there, and whichever project makes such a change owns the *Command routing* edit it implies. The brief's *Definition of done* is untouched, which is what keeps the amendment a scope boundary rather than an admission: this project still defines done only for the explicit design-state mechanism.
