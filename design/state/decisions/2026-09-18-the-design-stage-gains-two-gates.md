# decision/2026-09-18-the-design-stage-gains-two-gates
Date: 2026-09-18
Anchor: 2026-09-18 — The design stage gains a premise list, an approach gate, and a distribution section
Status: accepted
StatedIn: "unit/command/design § Premises, before anything is designed", "unit/command/design § Approaches, then stop", "unit/command/design § Distribution"

## Claim
`/design` stops twice before it writes anything. First a numbered premise list — three to six
load-bearing readings of the brief, emitted for agree/disagree, explicitly not a summary of it.
Then two or three approaches — minimal viable, ideal, and a lateral reframing where one genuinely
exists — with a recommendation, and the turn ends there; an obviously winning approach is stated
in one line and still stops, because the failure the gate prevents is writing the recommendation
into prose and carrying on until it is load-bearing in six sections. `## Distribution` joins the
required sections, before `## Failure modes` so that failure modes reach the install and upgrade
paths, and is required rather than conditional because a conditional section is answered by
omission and omission is indistinguishable from having forgotten.
