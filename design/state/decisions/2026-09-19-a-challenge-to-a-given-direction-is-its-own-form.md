## Claim
`AGENTS.shared.md` § *Working with me* gains a peer to the fork bullet for the case a fork does not
cover: a session concluding that the direction already given is the wrong one. It has the opposite
default — the user's direction stands unless displaced, and the burden of justification is the
session's — it is raised before the direction is acted on, and it is never resolved by the session
that raised it, because a challenge decided by its author is not a challenge. The mechanism is
adapted from `gstack`'s "User Challenge", whose trigger is two models independently agreeing; this
repository has no general two-model consensus to import, so the trigger is restated for a single
session and the burden moves into the raising rather than the gate being dropped. Two gates, both
required and both built from rules already in the file: the reason is cited as any finding is, and
the cost lands on the work — a broken invariant, a contradicted recorded decision, or a result the
stated goal rules out — rather than on the session's preference, so that "I would have done it
differently" cannot be raised as a challenge. Then five parts, all of them, of which the last two
are what make the burden real: what context the session may be missing, and what is lost if the
challenge is wrong and the original direction was right. The session also says which kind of
challenge it is, because a security or feasibility claim changes how quickly it must be answered,
and *A claimed limitation is a claim* already governs the evidence either kind owes.
