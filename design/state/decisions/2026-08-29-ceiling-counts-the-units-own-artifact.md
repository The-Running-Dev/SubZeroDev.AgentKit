Status: accepted

## Claim
`closure(U)` counts the unit's own artifact in full, alongside the active record and its one hop, and `design/00-brief.md` § *Definition of done* says so in the criterion. Absorption therefore becomes a strict saving: the artifact's bytes are counted whether or not a decision was written into it, so executing a decision into a site removes a record from the closure and adds nothing. The ceiling is not met on this repository's own corpus — the artifact outweighs the closure by roughly five to one on the largest units — so the brief's *Abandonment* line is live: the project stops and reports rather than relaxing the ceiling, and whether it proceeds, re-scopes, or stops is the human's.
